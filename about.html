The story behind the glow

UrbanLume started in a small converted garage in Brooklyn, where designer Elena Voss and artisan Liam Zhao began experimenting with reclaimed metals and borosilicate glass. Their vision was radical: create heirloom-quality lighting that never feels cold or industrial. Instead, each piece carries the warmth of human touch and the rhythm of the city that inspires it.

Today, our studio works with a collective of ceramicists, glassblowers, and metalworkers — all within a 50-mile radius. We design for longevity, repairability, and emotional resonance. Every UrbanLume lamp is numbered and registered, inviting you to keep it for a lifetime (or pass it forward).

Our guiding principles

Four pillars that illuminate every decision we make — from sketch to doorstep.

🕯️

Slow Craft

No mass production. Limited batches, hand-finished by artisans who take pride in imperfections that feel alive.

♻️

Circular by design

Modular components, repair-friendly assemblies, and a take-back program for future regeneration.

🤝

Radical transparency

We share material origins, carbon footprint data, and fair wage certifications for every collection.

🌿

Biophilic ethos

Light that mimics natural rhythms — dimmable warmth, glare-free diffusion, and organic forms.

Climate-positive by 2030

Every UrbanLume purchase includes a tree planted through our reforestation partner, Canopy & Roots. Our packaging is 100% plastic-free, and we offset 2x the carbon from every shipment. We’re also transitioning to 100% recycled aluminum for our pendants — coming this fall.
